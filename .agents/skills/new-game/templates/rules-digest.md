## R-1 Components and setup

Every component with its count by player count, every track with its values, the setup sequence in order.

## R-2 Turn structure

The round, the turn, the phases inside a turn, turn order and how it changes, what ends a round.

## R-3 Actions

One subsection per action a player may take: its cost, its preconditions, its effect, in the order the rulebook resolves it.

## R-4 <Mechanism>

One section per mechanism the rulebook treats separately (combat, auctions, markets, movement, card play), each in resolution order.

## R-5 Information

What is public, what each player knows, what is hidden from everyone, and when hidden things are revealed.

## R-6 Game end and scoring

Every end condition, the final sequence, every source of points, and tie-breaks.

## R-7 Component text

Only when card or tile text carries rules: one subsection per component kind, the text itself living in `components.md`, and here the timing words and keywords those texts use, each defined once.
